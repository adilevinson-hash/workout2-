💡 Strength & Size Progression

  • • Big compound lifts (squat, bench, deadlift, rows): add 2.5kg when you hit all reps with solid form
  • • Each muscle gets hit 3× per week (Full Body + KB overlap) — optimal for growth
  • • Full Body days = heavy (6-8 reps) for strength. KB days = moderate for endurance and cardio
  • • Face pulls appear 3× per week — your shoulders need this to handle the pressing volume
  • • At 49, recovery is king — the 2 rest days are non-negotiable, sleep 7-8 hrs
  • • Deload every 6-8 weeks: reduce all weights by 40% for one full week
  • • Eat 1.6-2g protein per kg bodyweight (128-160g/day at 80kg) for muscle growth
  • • Schedule: Sun-Mon-Tue-Wed-Thu or any 5 consecutive days with 2 rest days
